The tools I reach for every day share a quiet quality: they launch instantly, behave predictably, and never compete for my attention.

Whether it is a terminal utility, a note archive, or a personal web page, small improvements in latency and clarity compound over thousands of uses. Speed is not merely a technical benchmark—it changes how willing you are to open a tool and finish a thought.

Keep the surface small. Remove what does not earn its place. Let the work speak clearly.
